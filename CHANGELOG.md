## 0.1.0

Current-version baseline documented on 2026-09-29; this is not a new release.

- Control Logitech Litra Beam lights over USB and Bluetooth, individually or in sync.
- Respect device-reported power limits for brightness and support color-temperature controls.
- Provide camera preview, camera-driven light automation, and configurable Bluetooth discovery.
